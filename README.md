# Junyan Deng · Selected Work

A portfolio of audience research, editorial tools and short film.

- **After the Medal** studies English and Chinese Wikipedia readership around the Paris 2024 medal finals of three Hong Kong athletes. [Report](assets/after-the-medal.pdf) · [SQL analysis](assets/analysis.sql)
- **Signal Path** uses the Vivian Kong case to demonstrate source checks, time-series analysis and an evidence-led draft. [Case study](signal-path.html)
- **Prompt Architect** turns an editorial brief into a WeChat cover concept. [Case study](prompt-architect.html)
- **CreatorAI Lab** explores Instagram treatments and develops a data carousel. [Case study](creator-ai-lab.html)
- **A Place to Belong** is a short film that received a Lanqiao Cup National Final Third Prize. [Certificate](lanqiao-certificate.html)
